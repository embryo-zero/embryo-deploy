# Embryo Deploy

Embryo Deploy is Embryo-Zero's fork of [coolify](https://github.com/coollabsio/coolify) (4099500, commit 4099500). It does one-click app hosting on our machines.

- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
